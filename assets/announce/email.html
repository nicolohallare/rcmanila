The Heritage Library: 107 years of the Club, in its own words and pictures

Dear fellow Rotarians,

The bound volumes of the Rotary Balita, from 1948 to 2019, are now on the Club’s website, page by page and searchable, together with photographs of our projects and the plaques and trophies in our trophy room. To make this history easier to explore, the library now has a few new rooms:

🔎 Find a name

Type any name and see every Balita page where it was printed since 1948, year by year. Try your own, your father’s or your grandfather’s. Find a name

🗓 The Club’s timeline

From Leon J. Lambert’s first meeting in 1919 to today, each moment linked to the page where it was reported. See the timeline

🏛 October exhibit: The Club and the fight against polio

For World Polio Day on October 24: from leg braces for polio patients in 1950 to the End Polio motorcade of 2025. A new exhibit every month. Visit the exhibit

💬 Ask the archive

Ask a question about the Club’s history and get a short answer drawn from the Balita, with links to the pages. Ask a question

📷 Who’s in this photo?

Recognize someone in an old photo or Balita page? Tell us who it is. The librarian checks every suggestion before it appears.

Each week, the host will also have a one-minute history story from the Balita of the same week in an earlier year.

If you find a mistake, or have old Balita issues, programs or photographs at home, please let the Secretariat know.
