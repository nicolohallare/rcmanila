    Trophy room: plaques, trophies and medals

    ✓Finished. All 452 objects are in the online trophy room. Use this only to add newly photographed objects. Print QR labels is below.

    Step 1 (Start) makes a web copy of each photographed plaque or trophy. Step 2 (Polish photos and read labels) cuts each object out of its background, sets it on the gallery backdrop, and reads the text printed on it for a clean title, the giver and the year. It takes about 15 seconds an item; the original photos are kept. Then check a few and click Publish all.

    Bound Balita volumes, 1948–2009

    ✓Finished. All bound volumes are in the library. Use this only to add a newly scanned volume or redo one.
    Straightens each cover photo and trims the white background around it. The originals are kept.

    Each volume is read page by page: a web image of every page, the page text (read by machine where the scan has none), and the issues found inside. Pages that print home addresses have those lines blanked; member directories are withheld. Volumes with the same issues are grouped; the best copy is ticked.

    A volume takes about 5 to 20 minutes. The whole shelf can take a few nights. Use a computer that stays on, with a good connection.

    Video room

    Films and recordings shown at rcmanila.org/library/videos. Check the title and the one-line description, then publish. Videos marked Held for a check are full meetings or unnamed speeches: watch them first.

      Add a new video from this computer, for example the induction video from the Secretariat

      Use videos the Club made or was given. No commercial music, and only people who agreed to be filmed in close-up; no children from outreach events without consent.

      Albums to check before publishing

      Albums from outreach events (medical missions, schools, homes for the aged, relief work) are prepared but not shown until someone has looked through them. Publish only when no child or patient can be identified, or the Club has consent.

      Club timeline

      Draft entries were written from the Balita archive and the presidents’ profiles. Read each one, fix the words if needed, and publish it. Only published entries appear on the website (the timeline and each decade page).

        Monthly exhibits

        One story from the archive each month, shown on the library and home pages. The current month’s published exhibit is the one featured.

          Who’s in this photo?

          Names suggested by visitors for people in album photos and Balita pages. Approve only when you are confident; you can correct the names first. Approved names appear under the photo or page.

            History minutes

            A one-minute story from the Club’s past for each weekly meeting, from the Balita of the same week in an earlier year. The Secretariat sees this week’s on their Meetings page, and it is at rcmanila.org/library/minute.
